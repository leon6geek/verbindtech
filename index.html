How we work

Six steps. No surprises on delivery day.

Every machine follows the same route — and you see it run before it leaves our workshop.

Discuss your project
  1. 01

    Requirement workshop

    We gather every requirement — product, output, space, utilities, standards — before proposing a machine.

  2. 02

    Concept & 3D model

    Your requirement becomes a 3D model and layout you can review with your team.

  3. 03

    Proposal & design review

    We recheck the design against proven practice and freeze an approved drawing with you.

  4. 04

    Build or source

    Custom machines are built and assembled in Indonesia; standard machines come from our partner network.

  5. 05

    Test run with you

    Every machine is checked and test-run at our Cikarang workshop with you before it ships.

  6. 06

    Install, commission & support

    We install and commission on site, then stay on for after-sales service and spare parts.

Typical custom build: 1–2 months of production after the drawing is approved.

Buyer’s guide

Buying an industrial machine? Check these five things first.

An industrial machine is not a home appliance: when it stops, your deliveries stop. Here is what to ask any supplier — including us.

  1. 1Is the supplier experienced with your process?

    A supplier who understands the whole flow — raw material to finished pack — can tell you up front whether a machine will really deliver your shape, size and quality.

    Verbindtech: Since 2008 across pharma, food, FMCG, chemical and automotive.

  2. 2Will spare parts be there when you need them?

    Parts should be replaced before they fail, according to their service life — which means they must be available locally.

    Verbindtech: Partner parts plus local machining and fabrication in Jababeka.

  3. 3Do you know the full specification?

    Dimensions, power, space, speed, materials and the whole process must be clear before you buy — not when the machine arrives.

    Verbindtech: Requirement workshop, 3D model and approved drawing before production.

  4. 4Has the machine been tested?

    Insist on seeing the machine run with your product before it is shipped to your plant.

    Verbindtech: Test run with you at our Cikarang workshop before delivery.

  5. 5What does the warranty really cover?

    No machine is perfect. A warranty backed by a team that knows the machine is what protects your production.

    Verbindtech: 1-year mechanical & electrical warranty on machines we build.
