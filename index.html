01About Verbindtech

From a spare part to a complete production line.

Verbindtech Machinery provides customized industrial solutions for manufacturers across Indonesia, with a focus on West Java. We specialise in process technology — from spare-part manufacturing and stainless steel fabrication to complete special purpose machines — coupled with a first-class network of global technology partners.

Whether it is a new investment based on a feasibility study or an upgrade to get more performance from an existing facility, our engineering operations and technical services are always tailored to your needs, your site and the way you operate.

07Continuity timeline

Grown one workshop, one customer at a time.

  1. 2008

    Trimitra Jaya Mandiri

    The group begins supporting general items and special purpose machines, with its first two years focused on the automotive industry.

  2. 2010

    Trimitra Engineering Perkasa

    Growing demand for precision parts gives rise to TEP — supporting spare-part makers, jigs and heavy industry.

  3. 2012

    Mitra Cipta Teknika & TWU

    MCT starts fabrication and installation, beginning with a pharma powder process. TWU retrofits pharma and food machines — Manesty, Bausch+Ströbel, Glatt, Diosna.

  4. 2014

    Automotive focus

    TWU concentrates on the automotive industry as a special purpose machine maker.

  5. 2016

    Tanks, systems & AGRU

    MCT begins tank and system fabrication and jig paint removal at a new plant in Setu. AGRU starts building special purpose machines for pharma and FMCG.

  6. 2019

    Jababeka plant

    A new plant in Jababeka, Cikarang, ready for stainless and non-stainless steel fabrication.

  7. 2020

    Verbindtech Machinery

    Established for standard machines and trading, with designs standardised toward Industry 4.0 and wider industry support.
