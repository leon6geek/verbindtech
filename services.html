What we do

Six services across the machine lifecycle.

  • Installation & commissioning

    We install, start up and commission on your site, and test machines with you before handover.

  • Preventive & predictive maintenance

    Planned maintenance based on TPM practice, with parts replaced before they fail — not after.

  • Corrective maintenance

    A fast-response team for breakdowns, backed by the engineers who designed the machine.

  • Retrofit & upgrade

    New controls, drives and modifications that extend the life and output of existing machines.

  • Spare parts & fabricated parts

    Partner spare parts plus locally machined and fabricated parts when originals are slow or unavailable.

  • Building facility maintenance

    Support for the utilities and facilities around your production area.
