Solutions

Four ways to work with us — one team behind all of them.

Buy a proven standard machine, commission a special machine for one job, or hand us a complete line. Fabrication and service are there whenever you need them.

Turnkey process lines Special purpose machines Process & packaging equipment Fabrication & precision parts

01 · Turnkey process lines

A complete line, from raw material to filled pack.

We take responsibility for the whole process: we design the flow, select and build the equipment, install and pipe it, and commission it on your site. Design work is referenced to cGMP and laid out as a closed-loop system where your product needs protecting.

  • Process designRequirement, flow and 3D layout
  • Manufacture & supplyBuilt in Cikarang or sourced from partners
  • InstallationPlatforms, piping and utilities
  • CommissioningStarted up and proven with your product

02 · Special purpose machines

When no catalogue machine fits, we design one that does.

Special purpose machines have been our core since 2008. We start from your part, product and cycle time, model the solution in 3D, agree the drawing with you, then build, test and install it.

Automotive & industrial

Inspection stations, assembly jigs, press auto-feeders, quick die change (Dandori) and energy-free material handling.

Pharma & FMCG

Ampoule feeders, case packers, bottle cleaners, hot filling and cooling, IBC blenders with full safety systems.

Our own workshop in Jababeka, Cikarang.
