Company

Engineering partner to Indonesian industry since 2008.

What began as support for general items and special purpose machines has grown into a group that designs, fabricates, supplies and services machinery for some of Indonesia’s best-known manufacturers.

Who we are

Customised industrial solutions, tailored to how you operate.

Verbindtech Machinery provides a comprehensive portfolio of customised industrial solutions for national clients, with a focus on West Java. We specialise in process technology — from spare-part making and stainless steel fabrication to special purpose machines — coupled with a first-class network of global technology partners.

Whether it is a new investment based on a feasibility study or an upgrade to get more performance from an existing facility, our engineering operations and technical services are tailored to your needs, your site and your operating conditions.

History

Grown one workshop, one customer at a time.

  1. 2008

    Trimitra Jaya Mandiri

    The group starts supporting general items and special purpose machines, first for the automotive industry.

  2. 2010

    Trimitra Engineering Perkasa

    Demand for precision parts gives rise to TEP, supporting spare-part makers, jigs and heavy industry.

  3. 2012

    MCT & TWU

    Mitra Cipta Teknika starts fabrication and installation, beginning with a pharmaceutical powder process. TWU retrofits pharma and food machines.

  4. 2014

    Automotive SPM

    TWU focuses on special purpose machines for the automotive industry.

  5. 2016

    Tanks, systems & AGRU

    MCT adds tank and system fabrication at a new plant in Setu; AGRU builds special machines for pharma and FMCG.

  6. 2019

    Jababeka plant

    A new plant in Jababeka, Cikarang, for stainless and non-stainless steel fabrication.

  7. 2020

    Verbindtech Machinery

    Established for standard machines and trading, with designs standardised toward Industry 4.0.
