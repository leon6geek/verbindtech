Turnkey process line

Tea powder processing line

Challenge
A hygienic, closed powder process from raw-material dumping all the way to filling — designed to cGMP.
Solution
We designed, manufactured, installed and commissioned the complete line: dumping station, vibro screen, ribbon mixer, vacuum conveying, magnet trap, metal detector, IBC and a three-outlet auger feeding the filling machines.
Result
One accountable supplier from 3D model to commissioning, and a closed-loop system that keeps product contained.

Special purpose machine

Dandori multi-alignment press auto feeder

Challenge
Shorten die changeovers on a press line handling five dies.
Solution
An 11.3 m die-change system with roller carts for five dies, a preparation station, and movable and fixed transfer units in automatic and manual modes.
Result
Dies are prepared off-line and transferred into the press in alignment, ready for the next run.

Special purpose machine

Powerless scrap conveyor

Challenge
Move scrap to the scrap box continuously — without adding motors or energy use.
Solution
A straight and a lifting conveyor driven mechanically by a push-and-pull arm through a linear gear, bevel gear unit and universal joint.
Result
Continuous scrap handling with zero added electrical power.
