FAQ

Questions buyers ask us.

Straight answers for engineering, procurement and management.

How long does a machine take?

Custom machines typically take 1–2 months of production after the drawing is approved. Lead times for standard machines depend on the model and are confirmed in our quotation.

Can we see the machine before delivery?

Yes. We reassemble and test-run every machine at our Cikarang workshop together with you, and adjust settings until it performs as ordered.

What warranty do you give?

Machines we build carry a 1-year warranty on mechanical and electrical parts. For certain machines we include up to three after-sales visits.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Jababeka, Cikarang and serve Jabodetabek, West, Central and East Java and Yogyakarta, with clients also in Sumatra and Kalimantan.

Standard machine or custom build?

Both. Standard machines come from our technology partners; customised machines are designed, built and assembled in Indonesia. We will recommend what fits your process and budget.

What should we send for a quotation?

Your product, target output, container or pack format and available space. A URS, drawing or photos of your current line help us respond faster.
